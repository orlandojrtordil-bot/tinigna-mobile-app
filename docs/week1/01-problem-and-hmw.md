# 1. Problem framing and How Might We

## How might we

How might we help students give honest, safe feedback in the middle of the semester, so instructors can improve the course while the class is still running?

## Problem statements

1. End-of-term evaluations arrive after the course ends, so the students who gave them never benefit.
2. Students may hold back honest comments if they fear being identified or that it will affect their grades.
3. Instructors receive vague feedback, or none, and what they get is scattered across group chats.
4. Serious concerns (bullying, rule violations, safety hazards) should not go to the course instructor. They need to reach designated staff, such as the guidance office.

## Who is affected

- Students (2nd and 3rd year) who have feedback but worry about being identified.
- Instructors who want to improve mid-semester but only receive a few scattered comments.
- Guidance office or designated staff who receive student concerns.
